AI companies are snapping up RTX 5090 graphics cards by the stack, with prices already exceeding €5,000 – HKEPC

Andriy Rusanov Author of news articles for Mezha with four years of experience editing news feeds. I am a geek in everything I write about, but most of all in computers.
14 September, 15:00
8

The comparison between the AI and cryptocurrency booms is more literal than it might seem. For AI needs, companies are buying up not only specialized equipment, but also ordinary consumer RTX 5090 graphics cards, which are being repurposed for AI needs, says HKEPC.

In addition to the increase in the cost of RAM, such mass purchases are also contributing to the increase in prices. Video cards simply do not reach users. The capabilities of companies, apparently, exceed the capabilities of gamers in terms of price and quantity. Video cards are being unpacked and reworked: this involves adding RAM and modifying for multi-GPU or workstation configurations.

中國很多廠商會在各地收購 RTX 5090 並組裝成 AI 工作站出售。 pic.twitter.com/AssaXEylJ0

— HKEPC (@hkepcmedia) September 10, 2026
"In China, many manufacturers are buying up RTX 5090s in different regions, assembling workstations from them, and selling them," the Hong Kong publication writes.

Interestingly, the purchases are taking place in China, where only RTX 5090D graphics cards with limited AI capabilities can officially be supplied. Despite the sanctions, Chinese companies are finding opportunities to purchase regular RTX 5090s on foreign markets, which is confirmed by the photo.

VideoCardz notes that NVIDIA has the ability to enforce stricter rules on wholesale sales of video cards that can be tracked by serial number. But the publication "has not heard of NVIDIA preventing such companies from doing wholesale sales." Meanwhile, in Europe, the cost of a regular RTX 5090 has already exceeded €5,200.

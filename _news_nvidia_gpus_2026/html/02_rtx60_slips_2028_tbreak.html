Nvidia RTX 60 may be delayed to 2028, new leak claims

A newer leak reportedly pushes Nvidia’s next GeForce generation from 2027 to 2028, but the company has not confirmed an RTX 60 launch date.

Key Takeaways

  • A newer Kepler_L2 leak reportedly expects Nvidia's RTX 60 series in 2028.
  • The claim contradicts an earlier report pointing to the first half of 2027.
  • Nvidia has not confirmed the consumer roadmap or a UAE release date.

The Nvidia RTX 60 delay could push the next GeForce generation to 2028, according to a newer leak that backs up earlier reports. The claim comes from leaker Kepler_L2 in an AnandTech forum post, as reported by TechSpot and VideoCardz.

The report is not an Nvidia announcement. It is a second unverified signal about the same launch window, arriving after an earlier claim that Nvidia’s RTX 60 cards could launch in the first half of 2027. Kepler_L2 reportedly dismissed that timeline and said almost the entire next-generation gaming GPU stack from Nvidia and AMD is now expected in 2028.

Nvidia RTX 60 delay: what the newer leak says

Kepler_L2’s post suggests the RTX 60 series has moved from a likely 2027 launch to 2028. The leaker reportedly made the comment in response to a question about next-generation GPU timing, with ongoing AI accelerator demand cited as the wider capacity problem in the coverage.

Nvidia has not confirmed an RTX 60 launch date, specifications or even the final retail naming beyond the industry shorthand. The company has publicly discussed its Vera Rubin data-centre platform, but has not announced equivalent consumer GeForce hardware for that generation.

The new report also conflicts with a recent Moore’s Law Is Dead claim that Nvidia was preparing an RTX 60 launch in the first half of 2027. Neither leak establishes a firm schedule, and the difference between the two forecasts is a reminder that the consumer roadmap remains off Nvidia’s public calendar.

What the RTX 60 release date could mean for GPU buyers

The delay would leave the GeForce RTX 50 series as Nvidia’s current consumer generation for longer. It could also give AMD more room if its next Radeon cards arrive first, although TechSpot’s reporting says AMD board partners have also given mixed estimates, ranging from mid-to-late 2027 to early 2028.

The only AMD exception in the latest report is a chip referred to as AT2, which is still reportedly on track for 2027. That is a separate rumour rather than confirmation of a wider Radeon launch, and neither company has published consumer launch dates for these generations.

For now, Nvidia RTX 60 delay reports should be treated as roadmap leaks, not buying advice. Nvidia’s current cards remain the products available to shoppers, while the next generation has no confirmed UAE price or release date. Tbreak’s earlier report on the RTX 60 timeline covers the previous claim and its original 2027-to-2028 shift. The reported RTX 5090 price discussion provides context for Nvidia’s current high-end generation.

When could Nvidia’s RTX 60 series launch?

The latest leak reportedly places Nvidia’s RTX 60 series in 2028, but Nvidia has not confirmed a launch window.

Is the RTX 60 delay confirmed by Nvidia?

No. The 2028 timing comes from unverified third-party reports and is not an official Nvidia announcement.

What is the current Nvidia GeForce generation?

The GeForce RTX 50 series is Nvidia’s current consumer GPU generation, based on the information available in the reports.
